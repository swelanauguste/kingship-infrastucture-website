Connecting people.
Creating possibilities.

From the roads we travel to the ports that connect us to the world, infrastructure is part of everyday life. Our focus is on a safer, more connected and resilient Saint Lucia.

Discover what we do

Our mission

We support Saint Lucia’s sustainable social and economic development by strengthening:

  • The quality of roads and transport connections
  • Access to modern worldwide communications
  • The standard of public utilities
  • The readiness and resources of weather services
  • Responsive regulation and professional teams that serve customers and stakeholders

Our vision

To be a flagship Ministry critical to the achievement of infrastructural and national development.
